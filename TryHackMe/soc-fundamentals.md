SOC Fundamentals and SIEM Alert Investigation

1. Introduction

I studied Security Operations Centre (SOC) fundamentals through TryHackMe. I learned about the structure, responsibilities, people, processes and technologies involved in security operations.

A SOC primarily focuses on detecting, analysing and responding to cybersecurity threats and incidents.

2. SOC Team Structure

I learned about the different roles within a SOC environment, including:

* Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
* SOC Manager
* SOC Analyst Level 1
* SOC Analyst Level 2
* SOC Analyst Level 3
* Security Engineer
* Detection Engineer

These roles contribute to an organisation’s overall security monitoring, investigation, detection and response capabilities.

3. The Three Pillars of a SOC

The three fundamental pillars of a SOC are People, Processes and Technology.

A. People

People are the individuals responsible for operating the SOC and carrying out security activities.

The SOC team works together to monitor systems, detect suspicious activities, investigate alerts and respond to security incidents.

B. Processes

Processes are the procedures and methods followed by the SOC team when handling security events and incidents.

I learned about the Five Ws used when investigating an incident:

1. Who: Who was involved?
2. What: What happened?
3. When: When did it happen?
4. Where: Where did it happen?
5. Why: Why did it happen?

These questions help analysts gather relevant information and understand security incidents.

C. Technology

Technology refers to the security tools used by SOC analysts to monitor, detect, investigate and respond to threats.

Some of the tools and technologies I encountered include:

* SIEM: Security Information and Event Management.
* EDR: Endpoint Detection and Response.
* IDS: Intrusion Detection System.
* IPS: Intrusion Prevention System.

4. Practical Exercise: SIEM Alert Investigation

I participated in a simulated SOC investigation using a SIEM environment on TryHackMe.

During the exercise, a security alert was triggered, requiring me to investigate the incident.

Investigation Findings

The investigation involved identifying the Five Ws of the incident.

I discovered that the activity involved port scanning.

The simulated scenario also provided context indicating that the team had communicated its intention to perform the port scanning before carrying it out.

This demonstrated the importance of understanding the context surrounding an alert rather than automatically treating every suspicious activity as a malicious attack.

Outcome

I successfully completed the investigation and retrieved the CTF flag.

5. Key Lessons Learned

* A SOC focuses primarily on threat detection and incident response.
* People, Processes and Technology are the three pillars of a SOC.
* SIEM solutions help security analysts monitor events and investigate alerts.
* The Five Ws provide a useful framework for understanding security incidents.
* Port scanning can be legitimate or malicious depending on its context.
* A security alert requires investigation before a conclusion can be reached.
